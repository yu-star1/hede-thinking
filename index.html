Don't trust the ads—verify it yourself with five criteria

Swap in any model, connect to any host, and run it once (lock in the standards before you run)

  • J1 Compliance rate: ≥80% of pilot-task thinking segments organized per the specification (tags present and semantically matching)
  • J2 No quality regression: first-pass acceptance rate no worse than baseline, with no rework caused by the thinking format
  • J3 Honest volume: direction and magnitude of the tag overhead recorded honestly (expected slightly positive, no threshold set)
  • J4 User final review: readability subjectively judged by the user through personal spot-checks (AI may not judge on its behalf)
  • J5 Self-check after model swap: after every model change, first-window measured skeleton compliance ≥0.80 and tag line count >0 counts as PASS—measurements prove that "compliance rate = model × whether the spec is loaded" is a product

    • Verdict rule: J1∧J2∧J4 all pass = viable; judge once a sample of 3-5 tasks is complete; archive FAIL honestly, no changing thresholds to game the numbers.
